# Market size, growth and consumer trends: digital wardrobe / AI stylist / outfit-planning apps (France first, then UK/US)

Research date: November 2026. All figures carry their source and date. "Vendor estimate" means a figure from a pay-to-read market-report publisher. These publishers rarely disclose their methods, their numbers vary by about 10x, and they should not be treated as solid data.

## 1. Market size and CAGR (global, Europe, France)

### Takeaway
No credible independent firm (Grand View, Statista, Euromonitor, McKinsey/BoF) publishes a figure for "digital wardrobe apps" that I could find. All available figures come from low-transparency report vendors. They range from about $100M to $600M for "virtual closet apps" and from about $0.17B to $1.8B for "AI personal stylist", with CAGRs of 6–37%. The honest reading is that this is a niche consumer-app category, probably worth low hundreds of millions of dollars in revenue worldwide. No France- or Europe-specific figure exists.

### Cited Findings
- Virtual Closet App: $102.7M in 2022, rising to $209.1M by 2029 (10.7% CAGR). A second LP Information figure gives $208.6M at 9.9% CAGR for 2023–2029. Vendor estimate (LP Information, 2023). — [MarketPublishers / LP Information](https://marketpublishers.com/report/it-technology/other-ict-n-software/global-virtual-closet-app-market-growth-status-n-outlook-2023-2029.html); [MarketResearch.com listing](https://marketresearch.com/LP-Information-Inc-v4134/Global-Virtual-Closet-APP-Growth-35191802)
- Broader "Virtual Closet" market: $308M in 2024, rising to $469M by 2031 (6.2% CAGR; 6.6% in the 2025–2031 edition). Vendor estimate (GlobalInfoResearch). — [MarketResearch.com / GlobalInfoResearch](https://www.marketresearch.com/GlobalInfoResearch-v4117/Global-Virtual-Closet-Company-Regions-41376327)
- Virtual closet app: 14.5% CAGR for 2025–2032, reaching $855M by 2032. Vendor estimate. — [pmarketresearch](https://pmarketresearch.com/it/virtual-closet-app-market)
- Virtual closet: $612.4M in 2026, rising to about $2.48B by 2035 (16.8% CAGR). Vendor estimate, and an outlier. — [MarkWide Research](https://markwideresearch.com/virtual-closet-market)
- "AI-based personalized stylist": $171.9M in 2025, rising to $3.82B by 2035 (36.5% CAGR). Vendor estimate (InsightAce). — [InsightAce Analytic](https://www.insightaceanalytic.com/report/global-ai-based-personalized-stylist-market/1247)
- "AI personal stylist": $1.68B in 2025, rising to $7.92B by 2034 (20.1% CAGR). Vendor estimate (Intel Market Research). — [Intel Market Research](https://www.intelmarketresearch.com/ai-personal-stylist-market-43951)
- "AI-driven personal styling": $1.8B in 2025, rising to $9.7B by 2034 (20.4% CAGR), with North America the largest region. Vendor estimate (Dataintelo). — [Dataintelo](https://dataintelo.com/report/ai-personal-stylist-market)
- "AI fashion stylist app": $1.1B in 2025, rising to $4.8B by 2034. Vendor estimate. — [MarketIntelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/ai-fashion-stylist-app-market)
- "AI-driven virtual stylist": $1.82B in 2025. Vendor estimate. — [Growth Market Reports](https://growthmarketreports.com/report/ai-driven-virtual-stylist-market)

### Inferences
- The $1–2B "AI stylist" figures almost certainly include B2B styling engines (Vue.ai, TrueFit, Stitch Fix-type services), so they overstate the B2C wardrobe-app opportunity. The narrower "virtual closet app" reports are closer to the founder's scope: roughly $100–300M worldwide.
- The base is tiny, and no player has proven large subscription revenue. The real money in this category flows through adjacent markets (fashion e-commerce affiliate, resale, try-on), not through app fees.
- None of these figures should go in a pitch deck without the caveat that they are unverified vendor estimates.

### Gaps
- No figure from Grand View Research, Statista or Euromonitor for this specific category turned up in searches. Any such figure that exists is paywalled or absent.
- No Europe or France split was found anywhere.

## 2. Actual usage: downloads, users, interest

### Takeaway
The category leaders are mid-sized. Whering (UK) says it has more than 10M users after 5 years (July 2026), and Acloset (Korea) claims 7M+ users (undated). Both figures are self-reported. Independent download data (Sensor Tower, Appfigures) could not be accessed. The App Store holds many small AI-closet clones with 100K-class downloads, which signals low barriers to entry and a crowded long tail.

### Cited Findings
- Whering: seed round of $7M (£5.2M) led by eBay Ventures and the Google AI Futures Fund, with more than 10 million users worldwide claimed in the article dated 7 July 2026. The company was founded in 2021. — [TheIndustry.fashion, Jul 2026](https://www.theindustry.fashion/whering-secures-7m-to-expand-ai-powered-wardrobe-app/)
- Whering, self-reported user survey: 84% say they wear their clothes more often, nearly 70% say they cut fast-fashion purchases, and more than a third say they save £100–300 a year. — [TheIndustry.fashion, Jul 2026](https://www.theindustry.fashion/whering-secures-7m-to-expand-ai-powered-wardrobe-app/)
- Earlier Whering trajectory: 20,000 users in its first year (2021) and 4M downloads by May 2024. — [FashionUnited 2021](https://fashionunited.com/news/fashion/digital-wardrobe-app-whering-attracts-20-000-users-within-first-year/2021062940719); [Whering blog, 2024](https://whering.co.uk/thoughts/meet-the-global-whering-community)
- Acloset: "7M+ users worldwide, 90M+ clothes digitized" (company page, undated), up from more than 100K downloads in 2021. — [Acloset About](https://www.acloset.app/about/); [YourStory 2021](https://yourstory.com/2021/11/with-acloset-you-have-your-entire-wardrobe-on-your-phone)
- Alta (US, AI stylist plus digital closet, accounts for local weather and calendar): $11M seed round in June 2025 led by Menlo Ventures, with Aglaé Ventures participating. LVMH-linked backers are also reported. — [FashionUnited UK, Jun 2025](https://fashionunited.uk/news/business/alta-raises-11-million-in-seed-funding-for-ai-powered-personal-shopping-styling-app/2025061782265)
- Small AI-closet apps on the App Store (Closetly at 125K+ downloads, many others in the hundreds) show a crowded long tail of 2025–2026 entrants. These are store snapshots of unknown date. — [mwm.ai Closetly](https://mwm.ai/apps/closetly-ai-wardrobe-stylist/6744034749)
- Claims such as "64% of Gen Z use digital wardrobe apps weekly" come from an SEO blog (bestcolorfulsocks.com) with no methodology. Do not use them. — [example](https://bestcolorfulsocks.com/blogs/news/digital-wardrobe-management-statistics)

### Inferences
- Whering at about 10M cumulative users after 5 years is a useful benchmark for a well-funded UK leader. Monthly active users are probably a fraction of that, since wardrobe apps typically churn after the tedious item-entry step. MAU was not disclosed.
- Weather-adapted outfit suggestions are already a feature of funded competitors (Alta). Weather alone does not set the product apart.

### Gaps
- No Sensor Tower, data.ai or Appfigures category figures (downloads or MAU, by country) were publicly accessible. France-specific download figures for wardrobe apps were not found.
- No reliable survey was found measuring the share of smartphone users interested in a wardrobe app.

## 3. Consumer trends

### Takeaway
Demand drivers are strong. Outfit content is among the biggest content genres on TikTok, the "wear what you own" and resale wave is mainstream (especially in France, where Vinted is the #1 clothing seller), and weather is a recognised driver of outfit choice. The "matching outfits with friends" trend is documented only qualitatively.

### Cited Findings
**TikTok outfit content**
- #GRWM: more than 157 billion views on TikTok (AP/Fortune, Nov 2023). — [Fortune, Nov 2023](https://fortune.com/2023/11/27/get-ready-with-me-videos-tiktok-157-billion-views-users-prepare-dates-getting-fired)
- #OOTD: 163.3 billion views on TikTok (Dec 2023). — [NSS Magazine](https://www.nssmag.com/en/fashion-alphabet/35070/ootd)
- Statista tracks the top TikTok hashtags by post views as of October 2025, but the figures are paywalled. Statista also notes that one view can be counted under several hashtags. — [Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1299851/tiktok-top-hashtags-by-post-views)
- TikTok France: 27.8 million users claimed by TikTok (Sept 2025), France's largest market in Europe, out of 200M+ in Europe. This is a platform claim, not a Médiamétrie measure. — [Ecommerce Nation](https://www.ecommerce-nation.fr/tiktok-depasse-les-200-millions-dutilisateurs-en-europe-dont-278-millions-en-france/); [TikTok newsroom, Sept 2025](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/200-mar-en-europe?lang=fr)

**Matching / coordinated outfits**
- Pinterest Predicts named a "seeing double" trend (coordinated outfits) for 2025, as reported by Women.com. — [Women.com](https://www.women.com/1772750/hollywood-bffs-nailed-coordinating-outfit-trend-2025/)
- Fashion Magazine ran an editorial on the phenomenon of "dressing the same". — [Fashion Magazine](https://fashionmagazine.com/style/why-are-we-dressing-the-same/)
- Percentages circulating online (for example "65% of influencers") come from SEO blogs and are unverifiable.

**Wear-what-you-own / anti-overconsumption**
- "Underconsumption core" went viral on TikTok in mid-2024. In a Bank of America survey of Gen Z (spring 2024), more than 50% named cost of living as their biggest financial challenge. — [Fortune, Jul 2024](https://fortune.com/2024/07/20/gen-z-is-underconsumptioncore-tiktok-trend-consumer-spending/); [SCMP](https://scmp.com/lifestyle/fashion-beauty/article/3274134/gen-z-are-buying-less-reusing-more-thanks-tiktok-viral-trend-underconsumption-core)
- US: 54% of Gen Z shoppers prefer second-hand when it is available (survey of 1,500 US adults, Sept 2025). — [FashionUnited UK, Sept 2025](https://fashionunited.uk/news/fashion/gen-z-pave-the-way-for-resale-fashion-going-mainstream-in-the-us/2025092984156)

**France: anti-fast-fashion law and resale**
- The French Parliament gave final approval to the law against "ultra fast fashion" (identical vote in the Senate, reported 29 June 2026). Its scope was narrowed to ultra fast fashion. It bans advertising, including via influencers, once implementing decrees are published, and sets eco-penalties rising to €20 per item by 2030, capped at 50% of the pre-tax price. The European Commission issued two critical detailed opinions on 29 Sept 2025. — [Euronews, Jun 2026](https://fr.euronews.com/2026/06/29/france-le-parlement-adopte-definitivement-la-loi-qui-sattaque-a-la-mode-jetable); [Ecommerce Nation](https://www.ecommerce-nation.fr/?p=127104)
- Vinted is France's #1 clothing seller (online and offline combined), ahead of Amazon (#2) and Kiabi (#3), with Shein at #5 and Temu at #24 (IFM, Q1 2025). Second-hand accounts for 10.9% of total French apparel revenue and 16.3% among 18–34-year-olds. Online accounts for 29.4% of French apparel revenue. — [FashionUnited FR / IFM, May 2025](https://fashionunited.fr/actualite/retail/vinted-est-le-plus-grand-detaillant-de-mode-en-france/2025051538119)
- Vinted group 2025: revenue of €1.1B (about +38%), GMV of €10.8B (+47%), net profit of €62M (-19%). The company was valued at €8B in April 2026. — [FashionUnited FR, Apr 2026](https://fashionunited.fr/actualite/business/vinted-atteint-1-1-milliard-deuros-de-chiffre-daffaires-en-2025-et-assume-une-baisse-strategique-de-ses-benefices/2026040941303); [FashionUnited FR valuation](https://fashionunited.fr/actualite/business/vinted-la-valorisation-bondit-a-8-milliards-deuros-apres-un-rachat-de-parts-massif/2026042841508)
- A figure of about 23M Vinted users in France (2025) appears only on a blog (Fripio) with no primary source. It is unverified. — [Fripio](https://fripio.app/blog/vinted-valorisation-8-milliards-2026)

**Weather-based dressing**
- UK Met Office research (2025): the main reasons people check the forecast are laundry (41.4%), general curiosity (40.6%), and planning outfits or outdoor activities (34.6%, a combined category). — [Met Office, 2025](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/about-us/news-and-media/media-centre/weather-and-climate-news/2025/laundry-and-outfit-planning-among-the-leading-motivations-behind-checking-the-forecast-research-reveals)
- A survey of 2,000 women (her.ie, old and undated) found weather accounts for about 35% of the daily outfit decision. Weak source. — [Her.ie](https://www.her.ie/style/a-womans-outfit-35-weather-14-plans-for-the-day-and-7-mood-65278)

### Inferences
- In France, culture and regulation push in the app's favour: "use what you have" plus resale. Hooking into Vinted listings (sell what you don't wear) is a more natural monetization fit than pushing new purchases. Whering's eBay Ventures backing and its focus on resale integrations point the same way.
- Caution: the anti-ultra-fast-fashion ad ban (including influencers) means affiliate links to Shein, Temu and similar platforms will be both reputationally and possibly legally risky in France.
- The "matchy with friends" mode fits the social and TikTok-native trend, but there is no quantitative evidence of demand. It is a hypothesis to test, for example by measuring engagement on TikTok videos about coordinated friend outfits.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 hashtag view counts for #OOTD, #GRWM or #matchingoutfits were accessible. TikTok Creative Center was not fetched.
- No Google Trends data was found on "tenue selon météo" or "what to wear today".

## 4. Demographics

### Takeaway
The core users are young women. Acloset lists Gen Z and Millennials as its primary audience, and the French second-hand and TikTok audiences skew toward 18–34. No published age or gender breakdown for wardrobe apps was found.

### Cited Findings
- Women made up 51.5% of the French population on 1 Jan 2025, 2.0M more than men (INSEE). — [INSEE](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/8999231)
- Among French 15–24-year-olds, 83% use social networks daily. Instagram ranks above Snapchat and TikTok in that group (Médiamétrie, via The Media Leader). — [The Media Leader FR](https://fr.themedialeader.com/?p=219473)
- In France, second-hand accounts for 16.3% of clothing spend among 18–34-year-olds, against 10.9% across all ages (IFM 2025). — [FashionUnited FR](https://fashionunited.fr/actualite/retail/vinted-est-le-plus-grand-detaillant-de-mode-en-france/2025051538119)
- Trend Hunter lists Acloset's primary audiences as Gen Z and Millennials. — [Trend Hunter](https://www.trendhunter.com/trends/acloset)

### Inferences
- Order of magnitude, not sourced directly: France has roughly 5.5–6M women aged 15–29, based on about 11–12M people in that age bracket. Verify this in the INSEE age pyramid tables before using it.

### Gaps
- No exact INSEE count of women aged 15–29 for 2025–2026 was retrieved.
- No age or gender split was found for wardrobe-app users. Neither Whering nor Acloset publishes one.
- No Médiamétrie MAU figure for TikTok among 15–24-year-olds in France was found.

## 5. Fashion e-commerce context (affiliate monetization)

### Takeaway
French e-commerce reached €196.4B in 2025 (+7%), but online clothing and footwear fell slightly (-0.5%). Fashion is still the #1 category by number of online buyers (24.8M). The total French clothing market is about €34.7B, with roughly 29% sold online. Growth is shifting toward second-hand and ultra-fast-fashion platforms, not traditional e-tailers.

### Cited Findings
- FEVAD: French e-commerce totalled €196.4B in 2025 (+7%). Clothing and footwear fell 0.5%. E-commerce accounts for 12.2% of retail, rising to about 30% in categories such as apparel. — [Ecommerce Nation / FEVAD](https://www.ecommerce-nation.fr/e-commerce-france-2025-chiffres-cles-fevad/); [FashionUnited FR, Feb 2026](https://fashionunited.fr/actualite/business/le-commerce-en-ligne-en-france-frole-les-200-milliards-deuros-des-ventes-en-2025/2026021140700)
- Fashion is the top e-commerce sector, with 24.8M online buyers (59.5% of online shoppers) (FEVAD). — [CB News](https://www.cbnews.fr/etudes/commerce-ligne-toujours-plus-achats-mode-habillement-tete)
- French clothing market about €34.7B in 2025. Asian ultra-fast-fashion platforms sell about €5B in France (FEVAD/IFM figure for 2024). Shein is the #1 online fashion site by volume (IFM). Source: CCI Paris IDF Enjeux n°270, 2026, figures as reported in a search snippet; verify in the PDF. — [CCI Paris IDF PDF](https://www.cci-paris-idf.fr/sites/default/files/2026-04/enjeux-270%20-%20commerce%20d%27habillement.pdf)
- Shein, Amazon and Temu together made nearly one-third of online apparel sales in Q1 2025, about 7% of total apparel sales. 36.4% of apparel sales were made on promotion (IFM). — [FashionUnited FR](https://fashionunited.fr/actualite/retail/vinted-est-le-plus-grand-detaillant-de-mode-en-france/2025051538119)
- UK and US: estimates conflict. UK fashion e-commerce is put at $29.6B (IMARC 2025) or $53.4B (ECDB 2025). US apparel e-commerce is put at about $145–195B in 2025 (various vendors). Fashion e-commerce penetration in 2023 was 23.8% in the UK against 10.7% in the US (blog source). — [IMARC UK](https://www.imarcgroup.com/UK-Fashion-E-Commerce-Market); [ECDB UK](https://ecdb.com/resources/sample-data/market/gb/fashion); [eMarketer State of UK Fashion Ecommerce 2025 (paywalled)](https://www.emarketer.com/content/state-of-uk-fashion-ecommerce-2025)

### Inferences
- The affiliate pool is large in absolute terms, but French online fashion is flat. Large-platform affiliate programmes (Zalando, Amazon) pay low single-digit commissions, so meaningful affiliate revenue would need very large volumes. Commission rates were not verified in this research.

### Gaps
- No affiliate commission benchmarks for fashion in France were researched.
- No reliable UK or US figures (ONS or US Census for apparel e-commerce) were retrieved.

## 6. Saturation and decline signals

### Takeaway
There are no signs of category collapse. Investment is actually flowing in: Whering raised $7M in 2026, Alta $11M in 2025, and Daydream $50M. The bigger threat is horizontal giants absorbing the "AI stylist" feature: ChatGPT added virtual try-on in Oct 2026, and Google has had AI try-on since 2025. Meanwhile the app stores are flooded with cheap AI-closet clones.

### Cited Findings
- On 1 Oct 2026, ChatGPT launched Virtual Try-On and Favorites in its shopping features worldwide. TechCrunch frames this as moving into fashion discovery, territory dominated by Pinterest and Google. — [TechCrunch, Oct 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/01/chatgpt-can-now-virtually-try-on-clothes-for-you/); [Retail Gazette, Oct 2026](https://www.retailgazette.co.uk/blog/2026/10/chatgpt-takes-on-fashions-fitting-rooms-as-openai-makes-major-play-for-shoppers/)
- Google launched AI virtual try-on in 2025. Sources disagree on the date (May vs July 2025, US first). Per eMarketer citing Ryder, 31% of digital shoppers who use AI shopping tools have used try-on or visualization tools. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/google-shopping-ai-mode-price-alerts-virtual-try-on)
- Daydream (AI fashion search founded by Julie Bornstein) raised a $50M seed round. Glossy (May 2025) describes product discovery as being rapidly transformed by AI. — [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/fashion-search-is-broken-how-ai-agents-are-rewriting-the-ecomm-playbook/)
- Older apps such as Cladwell and Stylebook still appear active on the App Store. No 2025–2026 shutdowns were found. A historical piece notes that virtual closets have repeatedly failed to reach mainstream adoption. — [Cladwell App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cladwell/id1140550878); [Finery article](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/14876344-finery-aims-to-succeed-where-other-virtual-closets-have-not)
- Long tail of 2025–2026 clones (Closetly, Outfitly, Robed, Smart Outfit and others). — [mwm.ai list example](https://mwm.ai/apps/outfitly-closet-studio/6737692771)

### Inferences
- The defensible angle is not "AI picks an outfit", which LLMs and Google now commoditise. It is the social layer (friends, matchy meetup mode) combined with a hyper-local weather routine and a French focus. That differentiation is real but unproven.
- The historical pain point is the friction of entering clothes. It is shrinking thanks to AI background removal and receipt import (Alta), but it is still the main churn driver to design around.

### Gaps
- No Google Trends time series for "virtual closet", "wardrobe app" or "appli garde-robe" was retrieved, so interest over time remains unconfirmed.
- No public retention or churn figures for any wardrobe app were found.
